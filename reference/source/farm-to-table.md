### Ingredients directly from farmers, growers, and fishers, ensuring traceability from sea and farm to table.

Our commitment at Baan Lao extends to environmental protection and sustainability.

We choose to source our ingredients directly from farmers, growers, and fishers, allowing us to trace each item from the sea and farm to your table and ensure the integrity of our food.

At the very heart of Baan Lao, is Chef Nutcha's Organic Rice Farm:

Tilled by water buffalo, free of pesticides and naturally cultivated, the 15-acre farm in Khon Kaen, Thailand has been in her family for 80 years.
