### The World’s First Sommelier-led Afternoon Tea Thai Experience

Available Saturday and Sunday Afternoons

Baan Lao Fine Thai Cuisine proudly presents the world’s first Afternoon Tea Thai Experience, an

exquisite culinary innovation inspired by the time-honoured traditions of Chinese dim sum, British afternoon tea and Royal Thai cuisine.

  


Guided by a certified TAC (Tea Association of Canada) tea sommelier, the sensory journey

features 12 meticulously crafted dishes served in three courses, thoughtfully paired with five

distinct teas from across Asia.
